Hi there 👋

I’m Alejandro Marin, an engineer gravitating between software development and management. I'm currently an Engineering Manager at a stealth startup in the aviation industry. Previously, I worked at [Oxide](https://oxide.computer); before that, I was at National Geographic, TrueAccord, Expedia, and Starbucks (via [EPAM](https://www.epam.com)).

I like to collect my thoughts here.

## About me

First, I love music. My taste goes from classic prog rock like [Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_(band)) to [Britpop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britpop) to [random, obscure noise](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdPCt5ZEf40). I started collecting records in 2013, when the vinyl resurgence was in full swing. I have a childhood memory of my father recording from cassettes from borrowed records, and I wondered what the format would sound like in the streaming era. [I am now at the 300+ mark](https://www.discogs.com/user/ouritual/collection) (and growing!). Most recently, I started learning modular synthesis with my [Moog Mother-32](https://www.moogmusic.com/synthesizers/mother-32/), and while I aspire to create ambient-like sounds, I enjoy making random noise that turns into happy accidents.

I love being in nature; from camping to hiking and kayaking, I take every opportunity to be outside, even in the cold Ontario winter.

Professionally, I started my career as a software developer, building enterprise systems and data integration, eventually moving into Engineering Management, and even though I don’t code as much as in the early days – and now with AI taking over some of it – I still build things in my free time.
